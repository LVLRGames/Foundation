## gd-retroterrain

Foundation Phase 0 was visually and conceptually informed by [NickToony/gd-retroterrain](https://github.com/NickToony/gd-retroterrain), Copyright (c) 2025 Nick Hope, distributed under the MIT License.

The reference demonstrates a regular height grid, quantized retro terrain, `SurfaceTool` mesh generation, optional smooth normals, vertex-driven surface appearance, and per-cell diagonal selection. Foundation reimplements the relevant geometric ideas behind an original data-first, chunked API; no source files, textures, shaders, or assets from gd-retroterrain are copied into this repository.

The reference project's MIT license is available at <https://github.com/NickToony/gd-retroterrain/blob/master/LICENSE>.
